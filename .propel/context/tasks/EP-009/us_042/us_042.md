# User Story - US_042

## Story ID
- ID: US_042

## Story Title
- Risk Score Integration & Reminder Escalation

## Description
- As a clinical staff member, I want to view no-show risk scores for each appointment on the staff dashboard and have high-risk appointments automatically trigger escalated reminders, so that I can prioritize outreach for patients most likely to miss their appointment.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. **Given** a new appointment is booked, **When** the booking is confirmed, **Then** the system calculates a no-show risk score (0-100) and stores it per FR-029 in the NoShowRiskScore entity per DR-013.
2. **Given** risk scores are calculated, **When** the staff views the No-Show Risk Dashboard (SCR-022), **Then** each appointment displays its risk level with visual indicators (low/medium/high).
3. **Given** an appointment is classified as high-risk, **When** the reminder scheduler runs, **Then** automated reminder escalation triggers additional reminders at closer intervals than standard (72h, 24h, 2h) schedule.
4. **Given** appointment history, **When** retained, **Then** all appointment records are stored indefinitely for no-show analytics and model retraining per DR-013.
5. **Given** the risk score dashboard, **When** staff sorts or filters, **Then** appointments can be sorted by risk level and filtered by date range.

## Edge Cases
- What happens when the risk model is unavailable? (Appointments proceed without a risk score; default medium-risk reminders are sent as fallback)
- What if a patient's risk score changes after initial booking due to updated history? (Score is recalculated on next model inference cycle; dashboard reflects the latest score)
