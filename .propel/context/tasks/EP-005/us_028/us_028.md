# User Story - US_028

## Story ID
- ID: US_028

## Story Title
- Multi-Channel Appointment Reminders

## Description
- As the system, I want to send automated appointment reminders through SMS (Twilio) and Email (SendGrid) at configurable intervals before appointments, so that patients are reminded and no-show rates are reduced.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. **Given** a patient has a confirmed future appointment, **When** the reminder scheduler evaluates at 72h, 24h, and 2h before the appointment, **Then** SMS and email reminders are sent via Twilio and SendGrid respectively.
2. **Given** SMS delivery fails, **When** the first attempt fails, **Then** the system retries once with exponential backoff; if still failing, it relies on the email channel per NFR-020.
3. **Given** the SMS gateway experiences 3 consecutive failures within 60 seconds, **When** the circuit breaker opens, **Then** SMS delivery is paused and the event is logged; recovery is attempted after 120 seconds per NFR-014.
4. **Given** the email service circuit is healthy, **When** a reminder is sent, **Then** delivery status (success/failure) is logged in the audit store for each channel.
5. **Given** SendGrid free tier limits apply (100 emails/day), **When** the daily limit is approached, **Then** reminders are prioritized by appointment proximity (closest first) and high no-show risk.

## Edge Cases
- What happens when both SMS and email circuits are open simultaneously? (Reminders are queued for retry; admin alert triggered)
- How does the system handle reminder for a cancelled appointment? (Reminder scheduler skips cancelled/rescheduled appointments)
