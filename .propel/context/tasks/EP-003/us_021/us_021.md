# User Story - US_021

## Story ID
- ID: US_021

## Story Title
- Walk-In Appointment Booking

## Description
- As a Staff member, I want to register a walk-in patient for a same-day appointment with optional new patient account creation, so that unscheduled patients are efficiently processed and tracked.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. **Given** I am authenticated as Staff, **When** I select the walk-in booking option, **Then** the system presents a patient search form and available same-day slots.
2. **Given** the patient exists in the system, **When** I search by name or email, **Then** the existing patient record is returned for selection.
3. **Given** the patient is new, **When** I click "Create New Patient", **Then** a modal opens for entering demographics and creating the account during the walk-in process.
4. **Given** I select a same-day slot or queue option, **When** I confirm the walk-in, **Then** the appointment is recorded with status "Walk-In" and the patient is added to the same-day queue in arrival order.
5. **Given** a walk-in is registered, **When** I view the queue, **Then** a toast notification confirms the walk-in booking with queue position per UXR-504.

## Edge Cases
- What happens when a patient declines account creation? (Staff proceeds with minimal demographic capture for visit-only record)
- How does the system handle no available same-day slots? (Patient is added to the wait queue with estimated wait time displayed)
