# User Story - US_019

## Story ID
- ID: US_019

## Story Title
- Waitlist Management & Auto-Notification

## Description
- As a patient, I want to join a waitlist for a fully booked provider and receive automatic notification when a slot becomes available, so that I can still book my preferred appointment without manual checking.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. **Given** I search for a provider and all slots are booked, **When** I click "Join Waitlist", **Then** the system enrolls me on the waitlist with my preferred date range and provider preference.

2. **Given** I am on a waitlist, **When** a slot becomes available in my preferred window (via cancellation, reschedule, or swap release), **Then** the system automatically notifies me via SMS and email.

3. **Given** I am notified of availability, **When** I access the platform, **Then** the available slot is displayed prominently with a quick-book action.

4. **Given** the platform supports 500 concurrent users, **When** multiple waitlisted patients are notified of the same slot, **Then** the first patient to book gets the slot; others see "Slot no longer available" per NFR-015.

5. **Given** I have a waitlist entry, **When** I view my patient dashboard, **Then** I can see my active waitlist entries with status indicators and the option to remove myself.

## Edge Cases
- What happens when a patient is on multiple waitlists? (Each waitlist entry is independent; notifications are sent for each matched slot)
- How does the system handle expired waitlist preferences (date range passed)? (System auto-expires stale entries and removes them from active waitlists)
