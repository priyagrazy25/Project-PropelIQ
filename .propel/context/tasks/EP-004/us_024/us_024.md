# User Story - US_024

## Story ID
- ID: US_024

## Story Title
- AI Conversational Intake

## Description
- As a patient with a confirmed appointment, I want to complete my pre-visit intake through an AI-guided conversational flow that collects my medical history, symptoms, allergies, and medications in natural language, so that I can provide my information naturally without navigating complex forms.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. **Given** I select AI conversational intake, **When** the session starts, **Then** the AI engine initiates a guided flow asking contextual questions about medical history, current symptoms, allergies, and medications via Semantic Kernel + Ollama (Phi-3-mini).
2. **Given** I provide a natural language response, **When** the AI parses it, **Then** structured data fields are extracted with confidence scores and displayed for my review within 5 seconds at p95 per AIR-Q02.
3. **Given** the AI cannot parse my response (confidence < 0.5), **When** 3 consecutive low-confidence exchanges occur, **Then** the system suggests switching to manual form intake per AIR-008.
4. **Given** the AI engine is unavailable, **When** I try to start AI intake, **Then** a notification banner appears explaining the unavailability and the system automatically switches to manual form mode per UXR-605.
5. **Given** the intake conversation completes, **When** I review the parsed summary, **Then** all extracted fields are displayed for confirmation with direct field-level editing capability.

## Edge Cases
- What happens when the patient's response is in a language other than English? (AI responds with "I can best assist you in English" and continues in English)
- How does the system handle token budget exhaustion mid-conversation? (Session gracefully transitions to summary review with collected data preserved)
