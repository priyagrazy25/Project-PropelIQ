# User Story - US_026

## Story ID
- ID: US_026

## Story Title
- Intake Mode Switching & Summary

## Description
- As a patient, I want to freely switch between AI conversational and manual form intake at any point without losing data, and review a summary of all collected information before confirming, so that I have full flexibility in how I provide my medical information.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. **Given** I am in AI intake mode, **When** I click the mode toggle to switch to manual, **Then** all previously collected AI-parsed data is preserved and pre-filled into the manual form per UXR-103.
2. **Given** I am in manual form mode, **When** I click the mode toggle to switch to AI, **Then** all manually entered data is passed as context to the AI engine and the conversation resumes with existing data acknowledged.
3. **Given** intake is complete (either mode), **When** I reach the summary screen, **Then** all collected data is displayed in categorized sections (history, symptoms, allergies, medications) with click-to-edit on any field per UXR-104.
4. **Given** AI services become unavailable mid-session, **When** the circuit breaker opens, **Then** the system automatically switches to manual form mode with a notification banner per NFR-013.
5. **Given** the AI confidence drops below threshold, **When** 3 consecutive exchanges score below 0.5, **Then** the fallback to manual form is triggered per AIR-008 with all collected data preserved.

## Edge Cases
- What happens when mode switch occurs during an AI response? (Current AI response is abandoned; last confirmed state is preserved for manual form)
- How does the system handle summary edits that contradict AI-parsed data? (Patient edits override AI suggestions; confidence score for edited fields is removed)
